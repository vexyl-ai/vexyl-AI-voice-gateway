## Inbound Call Features

Handle incoming calls with intelligent AI that understands context and responds naturally.

- **Seamless PBX Integration** - Works with your existing Asterisk or FreePBX system
- **Multi-Provider Speech Recognition** - Choose the best STT for accuracy, speed, or cost
- **Intelligent Session Management** - Maintains full conversation context throughout the call
- **Natural Barge-in** - Callers can interrupt the AI naturally, just like a real conversation
- **Automatic Language Detection** - Routes to optimal providers based on caller's language
- **Human Escalation** - AI recognizes when to transfer to a live agent
- **Graceful Call Handling** - Professional call endings with customizable messages
